Our History

Gift of the Givers Foundation was founded on 6 August 1992 by Dr Imtiaz Sooliman, a medical doctor who qualified at the University of Natal Medical School. What began as a disaster response agency has grown into the largest disaster relief organisation of African origin on the continent, having delivered life-saving aid — including search and rescue teams, medical personnel, medical supplies, food, and water — to millions of people in more than 40 countries, including South Africa.

Over the years, the organisation has expanded into more than 20 categories of humanitarian projects, ranging from primary healthcare and water provision to education support, job creation, and feeding schemes, always guided by a commitment to serve without discrimination based on race, religion, colour, culture, or political affiliation.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To restore the hope and dignity of the most vulnerable in their time of need, rebuilding prosperous communities and empowering the leaders of tomorrow.

Vision

A continent where communities are equipped and empowered to recover from disaster and hardship without losing their dignity.
