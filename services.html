What We Do

Gift of the Givers runs more than 20 categories of humanitarian projects. Below are some of our core areas of work.

Disaster Response

Search and rescue teams, medical personnel, and emergency supplies deployed rapidly to disaster-affected areas.

Primary Healthcare

Establishment of primary healthcare clinics and medical support to hospitals in under-resourced communities.

Water Provision

Water purification and borehole projects to provide clean, sustainable water access to communities in need.

Feeding Schemes

Food parcel distribution and feeding schemes supporting families facing hunger and food insecurity.

Education Support

Bursaries, school supplies, and educational programmes helping learners stay in school and succeed.

Job Creation & Skills Development

Entrepreneurship support and skills development programmes to help communities become self-sufficient.
